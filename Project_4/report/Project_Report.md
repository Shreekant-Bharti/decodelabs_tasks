## Key Findings
Top insights:
- 1. Highest revenue product: Tablet (65814.56)
- 2. Best payment method (by revenue): Online (92430.49)
- 3. Best referral source: Instagram (91819.76)
- 4. Revenue trend over period: increasing (from 17951.68 to 18084.47)
- 5. Quantity trend over period: decreasing (from 146 to 146)
- 6. Top 3 products contribute 192782.49 total revenue; consider promotional focus.
- 7. Payment method share: 21.6% from top method — ensure smooth UX for this method.
- 8. Top referral sources: Instagram, Email, Facebook — consider allocating ad spend accordingly.
- 9. Revenue vs Quantity correlation: 0.01 — review pricing or bundle strategies.
- 10. Columns with missing values: couponcode — clean or impute before modeling.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing on top referral channels.
- Promote top 3 products with bundles and discounts.
- Improve checkout UX for top payment methods.

The visualizations and insights provide a data-driven starting point for commercial decisions.
